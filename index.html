02-CONTENT

What I Learned

Throughout Usrah Semester 1, I gained knowledge and understanding through discussions, activities and meaningful experiences.

Week 1

Self-Introduction & Hadith Jibril 1

In the first week of Usrah, we introduced ourselves and got to know each other. I was happy to meet new friends and enjoyed getting to know everyone better. Futhermore, we also began our first topic about Hadith Jibril. We had got a lot of knowledge. We learned about Islam especially how the prophet get the first revelation.

Week 2

Hadith Jibril 2

The second week, my friends and I got more close than before. Our Naqib also was so funny and his explanation was so clear and understandable. In this week, We learned about Iman and also Ihsan. Iman and Ihsan is important in every muslim. When we practice salah or other Ibadah sincerely, and we feel like Allah is watching us, it is called Ihsan.

Week 3

Pillars of Iman 1

At week 3, we learned about pillars of iman. Our session began with the first pillars,Belief in Allah. As a muslim, it is obligation toward us to believe to our creator,Allah. Futhermore, Belief in The Malaikah. Malaikah is created from Nur and Malaikah obeys to Allah's commands.Lastly, we learned further about Belief in Kutub which is Al-Quran. Al-Quran is the revelation from Allah to his prophet Muhammad.

Week 4

Pillars of Iman 2

Week 4, We continued our last topic, Pillars of Iman. We learned futher about Belief in Rasul and Anbiya'. After that, we continued to our lesson to the next pillar, Belief in The Hereafter. Lastly, we got more further about Belief in Qada' and Qadar.

Week 5

Day of Judgement 1

As a muslim, we need to believe to the day of judgement. If we dont believe to it we can be considered as a Kufr.
